How a check works

Four steps, each one a formula or a rule you can explain.

  1. 1. Clean the text

    Filler words are removed and synonyms such as car and automobile are grouped.

  2. 2. Find similar patents

    Text becomes TF-IDF numbers. Patents are ranked by cosine similarity.

  3. 3. Compare features

    Fuzzy logic gives every feature a score from 0 to 1.

  4. 4. Suggest improvements

    IF-THEN rules turn the gaps into suggestions and explain each one.
