About IdeaQuest

IdeaQuest helps people who have an idea for an invention check what already exists, and shows where the idea could be improved.

The patent data

IdeaQuest searches {{ "{:,}".format(stats.total) }} Indian granted patents from Lens.org{% if stats.first_year %}, published between {{ stats.first_year }} and {{ stats.last_year }}{% endif %}.

{{ "{:,}".format(stats.with_abstract) }} of them include an abstract. The rest have only a title, so matches for those are rougher. The patents cover areas such as vehicles, batteries, textiles, water, solar energy, agriculture, food processing, sensors and medical devices.

What to keep in mind

  • IdeaQuest only searches the patents in its data. It is not a live patent database.
  • It matches words and their synonyms. It does not understand meaning the way a person does.
  • It gives guidance only. It cannot tell you whether an idea can be patented.
  • Your idea is not saved anywhere.
  • Please check with a patent professional before you file.

Built with

Python and Flask for the server, and plain HTML and CSS for the pages. Similar patents are found with TF-IDF and cosine similarity, and suggestions come from written rules.
